Quick Start

The Calm 3-Step Method

You do not need a clever question. You only need to tell AI what you have, what you want, and how simple you want the answer to be.

Paste or describe the confusing information

Remove private details first, such as account numbers, full addresses, passwords, and medical IDs.

Ask for a simple explanation

Use one of the prompts below. Ask for short sections, bullet points, and plain English.

Ask what to check before acting

AI can help you understand, but important decisions should be checked with the original source or a trusted person.

Privacy pause: Before you paste anything into AI, remove personal details. You can replace them with words like [my name], [my address], [account number], or [doctor name].

Prompt Set

“Explain This Simply” Prompts

Copy a prompt, add your text, and send it to your AI assistant. These are written to produce calm, useful answers instead of long technical explanations.

The simplest all-purpose prompt

Please explain this in plain English for someone who is not technical. Keep it calm and short. Tell me: 1. What this is about 2. What matters most 3. Anything I need to do 4. Anything I should check before I act Here is the text: [paste the text here]

When the text is too long

This is a long document. Please break it into simple sections. For each section, tell me: - The main point - Why it matters - Any dates, costs, actions, or warnings - Questions I should ask if something is unclear Use short sentences and avoid jargon.

When you feel worried by the wording

This wording is making me feel worried. Please help me understand it calmly. Separate facts from possible concerns. Do not make it sound more serious than the text says. Tell me what I should check next.

When you only want the important parts

Please read this and pull out only the important details. I want: - Key dates - Money amounts - Required actions - Contact details - Risks or warnings - Anything that is just background information

Walkthroughs

Letters, Bills, Forms, Claims, And Articles

Pick the type of information you have, then use the matching prompt. The aim is to turn a confusing page into a small list of clear next steps.

Letter

Official Letter Walkthrough

Use for council letters, insurance letters, bank letters, appointment letters, or legal-looking notices.

  • Who sent it?
  • Why did they send it?
  • What do they want from me?
  • Is there a deadline?
Bill

Bill Or Charge Walkthrough

Use when you need to understand what you are being charged and whether anything looks unusual.

  • Total amount due
  • Due date
  • New charges or changes
  • Who to contact with questions
Form

Form Walkthrough

Use when a form asks for information in a way that feels awkward or unclear.

  • What each section is asking
  • Which parts are required
  • What documents may be needed
  • Questions to ask before submitting
Online

Claim Or Article Walkthrough

Use for health claims, money claims, product claims, news articles, and posts that sound dramatic.

  • What is being claimed?
  • What evidence is shown?
  • What might be missing?
  • Where can I check it?
Letter prompt

Please walk me through this letter in plain English. Tell me: 1. Who it appears to be from 2. The reason for the letter 3. What they are asking me to do 4. Any deadline or important date 5. What I should check before replying Do not assume anything that is not written in the letter.

Bill prompt

Please explain this bill simply. Show me: - The total amount - The due date - What the charges seem to be for - Anything that looks like a new charge, fee, or change - Questions I could ask the company if I am unsure

Form prompt

Please help me understand this form. Explain what each section is asking for in plain English. Tell me which parts seem required, which parts seem optional, and what information I should gather before filling it in.

Online claim prompt

Please break down this online claim. Tell me: 1. The exact claim being made 2. Whether evidence is provided 3. What information may be missing 4. What words are emotional or persuasive 5. Where I could check this from a more reliable source

Cheat Sheet

Jargon Translator

Jargon often makes simple things feel bigger than they are. Use this section to translate common phrases into plainer language.

Phrase Plain-English Meaning
Further action may be taken They may do something else if the issue is not dealt with. Check what action and when.
Outstanding balance Money they say is still unpaid.
Eligibility criteria The rules that decide whether someone can apply or qualify.
Supporting documentation Extra papers or proof they want you to provide.
Subject to approval It is not guaranteed yet. Someone still needs to say yes.
Terms and conditions The rules of the agreement. Look for costs, cancellation rules, and responsibilities.
Variable rate A rate that can change, often meaning the cost could go up or down.
Clinical evidence Research or medical information used to support a health claim. Ask where it comes from.
Limited time offer A sales message designed to make you act quickly. Pause and check if you really need it.
Data processing How an organisation collects, uses, stores, or shares your information.
Jargon translator prompt

Please translate the jargon in this text into plain English. Make a table with two columns: 1. The original phrase 2. What it means in everyday language If a phrase could have more than one meaning, tell me what I should check.

Trust Prompts

“What Should I Check?” Before You Believe Or Act

AI can make things easier to understand, but it can still be wrong. These prompts help you slow down and check the parts that matter.

Check the source

  • Who wrote or sent this?
  • Is it from an official website or account?
  • Can I contact the organisation directly?

Check the pressure

  • Does it push me to act immediately?
  • Does it use fear, urgency, or shame?
  • Does it ask for money or private details?

Check the facts

  • Are dates, prices, and names clear?
  • Is there evidence for big claims?
  • What would a reliable source say?
Trust check prompt

Please help me check this information carefully. Tell me: - What parts are facts - What parts are claims or opinions - What details I should verify - Any signs of pressure, urgency, or sales language - The safest next step if I am unsure

Scam-safety prompt

Please look at this message for possible scam warning signs. Do not decide for me. Just list anything I should check, such as unusual links, pressure to act fast, requests for payment, requests for personal details, or wording that seems suspicious.

Template

Step-By-Step Understanding Page

Use this page when a document feels like too much. Fill in what you can, then ask AI to help with the blanks.

My Understanding Worksheet

Worksheet helper prompt

I am filling in an understanding worksheet. Please help me complete it from the text below. Use plain English and do not guess. If something is missing, write "not shown". The worksheet sections are: - What this is about - Who it is from - Important dates - Money or amounts - What it asks me to do - Confusing words - What I should check - My next small step

One calm rule: If you are unsure, your next step can simply be “check with the original organisation using contact details from their official website.”

Keep Handy

Your One-Sentence AI Request

When you forget everything else, use this. It is enough.

The pocket prompt

Please explain this simply, tell me what matters, and tell me what I should check before I do anything.

Remember: AI is a helpful explainer, not the final authority. Use it to make confusing words easier to understand, then check important details before making decisions about money, health, legal matters, accounts, or personal information.
