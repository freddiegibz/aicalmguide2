Your departure file

Start here tonight

You do not need to fill in every page.

Start with the map. Put the important things somewhere other than your head. Then use the cards that apply to your family and trip.

This is not another packing list.
It is for the things that tend to turn into last-minute questions: who has the passports, where the medication is, when you need to leave and whether the house is actually ready to close up.
