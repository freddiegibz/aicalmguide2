### Mechanism

## The Everyday Ask Method: a simple way to turn “I do not know what to type” into a question ChatGPT can help with.

**1. Name the task in front of you**

Start with the real thing: a message, a letter, a plan or a decision. No imaginary exercises and no technical language.

**2. Add the details that change the answer**

Learn the few pieces of context that help AI give you something relevant instead of generic.

**3. Ask for the answer you actually need**

Ask for plain English, a gentler tone, a shorter version, more options, or the next sensible step.

The result: you can write your own useful questions, rather than relying on someone else’s exact prompt forever.
