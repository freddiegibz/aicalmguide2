# Calm beginner copy direction

## Who this page is speaking to

Someone who is curious about ChatGPT but uneasy around unfamiliar technology. They may be older, may have heard other people talk about AI, and may worry that they will press the wrong thing, ask the wrong question or look foolish.

They do not want to become an AI expert. They want a calm, trustworthy way to try it for ordinary life, at their own pace.

## The real job of the product

Help them move from: “I opened ChatGPT and did not know what to write” to: “I can use it when an everyday task comes up, without feeling intimidated.”

## Tone

- Calm, kind and unhurried.
- Plain English, with no assumption of technical knowledge.
- Reassuring without talking down to the reader.
- Practical rather than promotional.
- The 10 messages are a gentle first step; the full kit is the reassurance, guidance and confidence that makes that step feel safe.

## Page promise

> A calm, simple guide for anyone who feels unsure about ChatGPT. See exactly what to write, try it with familiar everyday tasks, and build confidence one small step at a time.

## Copy principles for this page

1. Lead with the reader’s nervousness and the reassuring resolution, not the number of messages.
2. Present the kit as a companion: it explains, shows, gives a first step and stays useful when they are unsure.
3. Mention the ready-to-send messages as “something to start with,” not as the whole reason to buy.
4. Use everyday examples to make AI feel less foreign.
5. Prefer “at your own pace,” “one small step,” “nothing to get wrong” and “ordinary words” over urgency, cleverness or performance language.
