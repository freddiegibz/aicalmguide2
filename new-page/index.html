AI arrived fast, but nobody gave us an instruction manual.

When AI first started appearing everywhere, I was sceptical. Friends were using it, family were talking about it, it was constantly in the news, and eventually I decided to see what all the fuss was about.

The problem was, I had no idea what I was supposed to type.

I would ask something ordinary, get a vague or disappointing answer back, try again once or twice, and decide it was probably more trouble than it was worth.

For a while, I assumed the people getting the most from AI were simply younger, more technical, or better at this sort of thing than I was.

So I stopped using it.

But AI kept appearing everywhere, and I kept seeing people use it for things that genuinely looked useful. Eventually curiosity got the better of me and I started experimenting properly.

And once I did, everyday life started getting easier.

Before

AI felt like something happening around me.
  • I didn’t know what to ask.
  • The answers were usually disappointing.
  • I still did everyday jobs the slow way.
  • I mostly listened when other people talked about AI.
  • I felt as though everyone else had somehow figured it out first.

After

AI became something I actually used.
  • Awkward emails took minutes instead of half an hour.
  • Long documents became quick, useful summaries.
  • Trips, research and comparisons were easier to organise.
  • Blank-page jobs became much easier to start.
  • I knew how to improve a weak answer instead of giving up.
  • I felt more capable, more current, and less dependent on other people to explain things to me.

The biggest surprise was realising that the gap was not what I thought it was.

I did not need to become technical. I did not need to understand how AI worked behind the scenes. And I certainly did not need to be younger.

I just needed to know which tool to start with, how to ask for what I wanted, how to improve an answer that missed the mark, and which everyday jobs AI was genuinely useful for.

Once I understood those things, AI stopped feeling like something important that was happening to everybody else.

It became a useful tool in my own life.

That is why I put the AI Confidence Kit together: to give other people the straightforward starting point I wish somebody had given me before I spent all those hours figuring it out for myself.
