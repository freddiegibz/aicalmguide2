The first people I helped with AI were not tech people. They were older people in my own family who had heard about AI, knew it was becoming important, but felt unsure where to start. What struck me was not that they were incapable. It was that nobody had explained it in a way that respected how normal people actually learn something new.

Once we sat down together, the change was immediate. When I showed them what to ask, how to judge the answer, and where AI could help in ordinary life, the fear started to drop. They used it for practical things across life, business, travel, and relationships. The best part was seeing the pride on their faces when they realised they could actually do this.

After selling more than 200 AI guides and personally working with over 30 people, I kept seeing the same pattern. Guides can help, but some people need a human being beside them for the first step. Not because they are behind, but because they have specific questions, specific worries, and specific places where they want AI to make sense.

That is why I created the AI Clarity Session. It is not a course, a technical training, or a pile of prompts. It is private, plain-English help for beginners who want AI explained through their own life, so they can leave with confidence, practical next steps, and the feeling that this is something they are allowed to use too.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

I am new to AI. Is this still for me?
+
Yes. This session is specifically for thoughtful, capable people who do not feel confident with AI yet. You do not need technical knowledge, special terminology, or a list of clever prompts before we begin. Basic questions are welcome.
What if I do not even know what to ask?
+
That is completely fine. Part of the session is helping you work out where AI could actually help you. You can arrive with confusion, rough thoughts, or ordinary tasks you wish were easier.
Why can I not just watch YouTube or use free advice?
+
You can, and some free advice is useful. The problem is that it is usually generic. This session is different because we apply AI to your own life, your own questions, and your current level of confidence.
I tried AI before and the answers were not very good. Will this help?
+
Yes. A lot of disappointing AI answers come from unclear starting points, vague questions, or not knowing how to follow up. We will work through how to ask better, improve weak answers, and spot when the response needs checking.
Will this be too technical?
+
No. The whole point is to explain AI in plain English. We will keep the session practical, calm, and focused on what you can use, not on technical theory.
Can I trust what AI says?
+
Not always, and that is part of what we cover. You will learn simple rules for when to trust, when to check, what not to share, and how to treat AI answers with a sensible level of caution.
Is the session done by text or call?
+
You can choose the format that feels easiest for you. If you prefer speaking things through, you can do it by call. If you feel more comfortable writing your questions out, you can do it by text.
What can we use the session for?
+
We can look at everyday admin, writing, planning, business ideas, travel, relationships, decision-making, understanding information, or any other area where you suspect AI could help but do not know where to start.
Do I need to install anything?
+
No complicated setup is expected. You will receive simple instructions after booking, and if anything needs setting up, we keep it basic and walk through it calmly.
Will you try to sell me software?
+
No. This is guidance only. The goal is to help you understand AI and use it more comfortably, not push tools, subscriptions, or technical systems.
Is one session enough?
+
One session is designed to help you understand the basics, ask your private questions, see useful examples, and leave with starter prompts and next steps. You do not need to become an expert for it to be useful.
What happens after I pay?
+
After payment, you will receive the next step to book your private session. You will then choose whether you want text or call, pick a time if needed, and bring whatever questions or situations you want help with.
Why is it priced at £49?
+
The price is intentionally accessible because this is built for people who want direct help without committing to an expensive course, consultant, or long programme. It is a focused session to get you unstuck.
